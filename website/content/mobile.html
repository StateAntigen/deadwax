DEADWAX COMPANION

Your music. Wherever you are.

Deadwax connects another computer or phone to your running Deadwax desktop. It creates a secure tunnel to stream data using Iroh. Deadwax lets you browse and play the music in your Deadwax folder and ask Deadwax to download a track while you are away from the computer.

How it works

  1. Open the Deadwax page in Deadwax and create a one-use QR code.
  2. Scan it with Deadwax, or paste the manual pairing code, to connect over encrypted Iroh.
  3. Deadwax continues to handle Nostr discovery and private Tor downloads.
  4. Verified audio already held by Deadwax reaches the paired phone over Iroh.

Deadwax must remain open and connected while Deadwax accesses your desktop library. Podcasts are independent and come directly from their public publishers.

Only pair yourself and people you really trust.

A paired phone can browse your indexed music, fetch its audio, and ask your Deadwax desktop to download tracks. Treat pairing like giving someone access to your music library. Remove a phone from Deadwax’s Deadwax page as soon as it should no longer connect.

The QR secret expires after five minutes and can be used successfully only once.
