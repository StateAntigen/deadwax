Guess Who’s Back!

Deadwax is bringing music sharing back with modern privacy. Searchable catalogues are published through Nostr while audio transfers travel through authenticated, temporary Tor onion services. There is no central Deadwax tracker and no central file store. Read about the Deadwax privacy model.

How Deadwax Works

Users share music from a folder, and their catalog is published over Nostr. Deadwax negotiates download requests through private Nostr DMs, while Tor handles the downloads, keeping users’ IP addresses private.
